Whoever is selected receives:

  1. An SMS right after booking — plot, estate, the 14-day window, and the estate's deposit threshold and required KYC docs.
  2. A daily reminder SMS on days 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 if the deposit threshold or KYC docs are still incomplete.

The buyer's day-14 "plot released, please share refund details" notice always sends regardless of this setting — it's tied to their money, not a preference.
